About Us

Built for the Serious Finance Candidate

No sponsored rankings. No admissions referral fees. Just rigorous, data-driven guides for people who want to get into the right program and build a real career in finance.

Our Four Pillars

📈

Data-Driven Analysis

Every article is anchored to primary sources — GMAC reports, school employment reports, Bloomberg data, and verified salary surveys. We cite everything.

⚖️

Editorial Independence

We do not accept payment to rank programs, feature schools, or place affiliate links in editorial content. Our rankings reflect data, not relationships.

📚

Practitioner Perspective

Our content team includes MBA graduates, former investment banking analysts, and finance recruiters who bring real-world context to every guide.

🔓

Accessible & Free

All guides are free, forever. Finance career information should not be locked behind paywalls. We are funded by non-intrusive advertising only.

Who We Are

MBA Finance Guide was founded by a team of finance professionals and educators who were frustrated by the lack of truly independent, rigorous guidance for MBA applicants and finance career seekers. Most resources are either superficial or monetized by the schools they claim to rank.

We built MBA Finance Guide to be the resource we wished had existed when we were navigating these decisions ourselves — clear, honest, and grounded in real data.
